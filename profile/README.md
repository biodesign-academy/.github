# Biodesign Academy

Insights and tools for learning and practising AI-native biodesign. We work with designers, students and educators who use proteins and living matter, and we try to keep care and fairness inside the work, not added afterwards.

Website: [biodesign.academy](https://www.biodesign.academy) · Based in Stockholm and London

## Tools

**[Protein Backstory](https://biodesign-academy.github.io/protein-backstory/)**, where your protein came from, and who it's owed to.
Paste a UniProt protein ID and draft a provenance note: the organism, where and when it was collected, who recorded it, what is missing, and who it may be owed to. Free, runs in your browser, nothing is sent to us.
[Use it](https://biodesign-academy.github.io/protein-backstory/) · [Code](https://github.com/biodesign-academy/protein-backstory) · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24441516)
